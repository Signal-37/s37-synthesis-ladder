# Governance

1. The S37 Synthesis Ladder is released under the Signal 37 (S37) name.
2. The ladder is J. Scott King's. He holds its copyright, and decides what changes and when it is released.
3. The files in this package are built from the author's own working copy by a deterministic builder, and are not hand-edited here. A change is made in the working copy and the package is rebuilt.
4. Releases are recorded in the Releases section of `LICENSING.md`, which is append-only. The version and build date of a copy are in its `VERSION` file.
5. Contributions arrive as issues (`CONTRIBUTING.md`). The author adopts what he takes up, with credit.
