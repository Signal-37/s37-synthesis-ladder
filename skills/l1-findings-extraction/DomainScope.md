# Domain Scope Reference

Defines what "domain implications" look like for each project type. The behavioral scope categories in FindingsFormat.md are universal — this file parameterizes only the higher-inference implication layer.

Confirm the domain at the start of ExtractFindings (Step 1). Use the matching entry below to frame the implication questions throughout extraction.

---

## brand/design

**Key decision-maker:** Brand team, design team, product team

**Implication questions:**
- What brand perception problem does this reveal?
- What purchase or adoption barrier does this expose?
- What opportunity does this behavior point to?
- What would the brand or product have to change to serve this person better?

---

## product/UX

**Key decision-maker:** Product manager, UX team, engineering

**Implication questions:**
- What usability problem or friction point does this reveal?
- Where does this person's mental model diverge from how the product actually works?
- What feature gap or missing capability does this behavior point to?
- What would the product have to change for this person to accomplish their goal more reliably?

---

## organizational

**Key decision-maker:** Leadership, operations, HR, change management team

**Implication questions:**
- What process failure or structural gap does this behavior reveal?
- Where is this person working around the official system — and what does that workaround cost?
- What cultural or organizational friction is shaping this behavior?
- What decision-making gap does this expose — who doesn't have what information, when?

---

## policy/advocacy

**Key decision-maker:** Policy team, advocacy organization, government body

**Implication questions:**
- What barrier to participation, access, or equity does this reveal?
- What unmet need is the current policy failing to address?
- What behavior is the policy producing that it wasn't designed to produce?
- What would the policy or system have to change to serve this person's actual situation?

---

## other

**Key decision-maker:** [Confirm with user at Step 1]

**Implication questions (generic — adapt to the project):**
- What gap or failure does this behavior reveal in the system being studied?
- What is this person working around, and what does that reveal about where the system falls short?
- What opportunity does this behavior point to?
- What would the system, organization, or offering have to change to serve this person better?
