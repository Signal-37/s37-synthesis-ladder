## Authorship

`l0-survey-corpus-prep` is J. Scott King's work: an extension of his synthesis ladder to open-text survey data, built after a trial run of the ladder on large survey exports showed a missing step between a spreadsheet and L1. Its scripts were written new for this edition.

## Sources

The saturation stopping rule adapts Guest, Namey & Chen (2020), "A simple method to assess and report thematic saturation in qualitative research," *PLOS ONE* 15(5): e0232076, from interviews and themes to survey rows and findings. The method is cited; none of its text is reproduced.
