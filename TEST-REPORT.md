## The v1.3 changes

  - A fresh agent on Claude Sonnet audited the seeded 23-theme set from the v1.2 test (10 planted defects, and about 20 natural ones known from an earlier audit), following the v1.3 L2 audit as installed from this build, 2026-11-17. It was told to make no fixes.

The gates checker read the gate line and warned, as it should for a set that has not cleared. The theme files were unchanged.
  - A separate agent scored the report against the defect list, under the v1.2 test's scoring. **Planted defects caught: 6.5 of 10**, against a mean of 6.92 for the same audits in v1.2: one run, inside the spread of those runs. The three it missed include the two no v1.2 run caught (a conditional rewritten as behavior; an interviewer's words inside a participant quote), which is known gap 1.

No Advisory action should have been Blocking. The defect list rates only 3 of its 10 defects as blocking, and the audit marked every planted defect it caught Blocking, so it still marks more as Blocking than the list's author would.
  - **Limits.** One run and one scorer. The scorer checked one or two cited findings for each action, not every claim. ## The v1.2 audit changes

- **Audits 8 and 9 at L2: a blind seeded test.**
  - A separate reviewer wrote 10 defects (8 found only by reading, 2 mechanical; 3 that should block clearance). The author approved them without the audit writer seeing them.
  - The defects were planted in an interim 23-theme set from a past engagement.
  - Each run was a fresh agent on Claude Sonnet, auditing it under the audit as it stood before v1.2 (arm A), with Audits 8 and 9 (arm B), and with B plus a reviewer-stance block (arm C). Runs carried shuffled IDs.
  - A separate agent scored every report against the defect list without knowing which arm wrote it.
  - **Six runs each:** A caught a mean of 4.83 of 10, B 6.92. The rule, fixed before the second round was scored, asked for B's mean at least 2 higher and 5 of its 6 runs above A's median. B passed, narrowly.
  - **Stance block:** arm C did not add planted catches at L2 (a mean of 5.8 of 10 against 6.8 without it, over three runs: a gap smaller than the spread of the no-stance arm's own runs) and made the audit stricter (on one run on a cleared set, 17 blocking findings, which the scorer judged 2 real, 15 defensible, 1 false). It is not included in the L2 audit. The test scored planted defects only, so it could not credit independent catches of natural defects, where the stance block had looked strongest in an earlier single run.
  - **False flags:** each arm was also run once on a cleared 25-theme set. B's false findings did not rise (2 against A's 3), and B found real residual defects there that A had cleared.
- **The pre-pass on real sets.**
  - **L2 (25 themes, 586 findings):** all 819 quotes present and all 795 citations resolved. Its uncited list matched an independent script exactly.
  - **L3 (10 insights):** 6 candidates, none a misquote.
  - **L4 and L5:** run on an older complete ladder from a past engagement. Every citation resolved at all five sets, and nothing failed to parse. The noise it showed was fixed before this build: authored archetype indicators, items marked absorbed, and items quoting their own text.
- **The L4 and L5 audits on real sets.** One fresh audit of the same older ladder's principles and one of its framework, under the v1.2 workflows. Both followed the Inputs step and ran the pre-pass. The framework audit flagged the older Falsifier lines against the new "What would change this" section, as it should. Two workflow sentences came from these runs: gate-check scope, and reading files with a file-reading tool.
- **The final ladder review's enriched Seat 1 brief.** One briefed Seat 1 review of the same older ladder ran with the previous brief and one with the enriched brief. A separate agent compared both, blind, against that ladder's earlier final review. Both found the same core findings, and each had two or three verified findings the other lacked. The comparison found the difference small and mixed, and one run each cannot separate the brief from run-to-run variation.
