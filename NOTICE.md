## Authorship and provenance

The L0–L5 synthesis ladder — the skill chain, the ladder map (`skills/ladder-core/ladder-map.md`),
and associated skills such as `abductive-reasoning` — is **J. Scott King's own work**, built in
his personal AI setup.

It was *inspired by* work J. Scott King and Jon Freach did together at frog Design in **November 2015**
(a whiteboard sketched under the in-studio working title "frog's Sensemaking Process"), and draws on
residual practitioner knowledge from Scott. **Jon Freach's 2015 thinking is
acknowledged as an influence, not as authorship of this build.**

The leveled ladder is **not part of the frog Design Research Toolkit or frog proprietary
methodology** — verified against the 2016 toolkit, which carries the observation → theme → insight
synthesis method and the frame/learn/make arc, but no leveled ladder, no raw-data floor level, and
no 0–5 numbering.

Level transitions are grounded in **Jon Kolko's** abductive synthesis theory; L2 pattern discipline
draws on **LeCompte & Schensul**; the L4 archetype scaffold uses the published **frog Consumer
Archetypes POV** (Morey, Teichgraeber, King, Darby, Pyrchla). These are cited influences and
sources, not co-authorship of this package.
